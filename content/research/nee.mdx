## Valuation

A two-stage dividend discount model: faster dividend growth for five years, then a fade to a long-run rate. At the 10/13 close of **$83.39**, our fair value is **$100.90** (+21.0%).

## What the price implies

Sustainable-growth check: ROE 11% × retention 42% = **4.6%**. That is below our stage 1 growth, so the early dividend growth relies on leverage and needs watching. The current price implies stage 1 growth of about 5.1%.
