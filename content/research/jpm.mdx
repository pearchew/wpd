## Valuation

Banks do not have meaningful free cash flow, so we use a residual-income model anchored on price to tangible book. At the 10/13 close of **$309.81**, our fair value is **$334.59** (+8.0%).

## What the price implies

At today's price the market is paying for an ROTE of about **15.0%**, below the five-year average of 17.5%. The gap to our 19.0% is the upside, and it is narrow, hence Watch.

## Biggest risk

Credit costs normalising while rate cuts squeeze net interest income at the same time.
