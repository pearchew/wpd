## What the price implies

The yield pays us for the duration risk only if rates stay put. The job of this position is diversification, not return: adding it at a 7% weight lowers portfolio volatility.

## Biggest risk

Inflation re-accelerates: bonds and equities fall together and the diversification benefit disappears.
