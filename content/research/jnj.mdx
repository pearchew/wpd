## Valuation

A two-stage dividend discount model: faster dividend growth for five years, then a fade to a long-run rate. At the 10/13 close of **$162.54**, our fair value is **$186.92** (+15.0%).

| Assumption | Base case |

## What the price implies

Sustainable-growth check: ROE 22% × retention 45% = **9.9%**. That comfortably supports our stage 1 growth. The current price implies stage 1 growth of about 4.5%.

Litigation outcome far worse than provisioned, or a patent cliff arriving earlier than modelled.
